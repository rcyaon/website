Welcome to my corner of the internet!
ECE at Olin. Grew up across Germany, Belgium, and Spain. Also always aiming to learn more about hardware!
Outside of that I love: Kiarostami films (check out my Letterboxd!), rock climbing, and music (I recently saw Julie, Geordie Greep, and LCD Soundsystem in concert).
Some things I've done:
  • Taped out a custom 8-bit SAR ADC in TSMC 180nm as part of Cornell's all-undergrad silicon team — one of the only teams in the country doing open-source chip design. It's now detecting bird calls on tiny backpacks strapped to migrating birds for Cornell's ornithology lab
  • Built a vibrating bracelet to counteract Parkinson's tremors out of 3D-printed plastic and exposed wires — rebuilt it 5 times, learned CAD, then PCBs along the way
  • Built AWS analytics pipelines at Amazon that cut anomaly detection from 8 days to under 24 hours and incident resolution from 7 days to 6 hours. Did this as a freshman
Got here through robotics competitions, teaching myself to code in a school that didn't offer it, and starting a club in my bedroom that somehow won a national cybersecurity competition. If any of this resonates, or you just want to talk chips/films/ham radio/climbing, reach out!
